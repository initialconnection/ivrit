# Ivrit Learning System Plan

One place to learn Hebrew from zero to reading real texts, tracking every word, root and skill along the way. It starts in English letters and gradually moves the learner into Hebrew script.

**Who it's for:** English speakers who want to set a clear Hebrew goal and a timeline to reach it (for example fluent in 6 months, 8 months or 1 year), starting even if they can't read Hebrew yet.

## Principles
- Transliteration first, script second, with the learner choosing when to switch
- Roots are the backbone: vocabulary, grammar and reading link back to them
- Everything connects: a word from flashcards shows up in sentences, then in texts
- Progress is always visible: what's mastered, what's due, what's next
- Daily time sized to the goal, with extra sessions encouraged
- Built in small steps: each phase works on its own

## Content areas
- **Letters & reading:** alef-bet, final forms, dagesh, nikud, reading ladder, its own tab and progress grid
- **Vocabulary:** word cards with transliteration, script, gender/plural, root, audio; spaced repetition
- **Roots:** root explorer, 7 binyanim, noun patterns, conjugation tables, root games
- **Grammar:** short lessons by level with cheat sheets
- **Sentences:** builder, translate both ways, fill the gap, phrasebook, gender toggle
- **Texts:** graded stories, dialogues, Jewish texts; tap-to-translate reader with script switch
- **Listening & speaking:** audio for everything, slow mode, pronunciation guide, record and compare

## Progress
- Per-word, per-letter, per-root and per-grammar mastery
- Overall fluency score (0–100% of the program) that rises with practice and slips with time away; comeback test after breaks
- Goal and timeline: pick a level and deadline, get a daily plan, milestones and on-track status
- Encouragement messages, streaks, badges

## Build roadmap
1. **Foundation** (done): data files linked by ID, per-word progress with spaced repetition, fluency score, goal and timeline, six-tab layout, backup
2. **Letters and reading** (done): alef-bet lessons, nikud, script switch, letter games, letter/vowel progress
3. **Words and roots** (done, more vocabulary batches to come): review queue, root explorer, matching and speed games, Aleph and Bet vocabulary
4. **Sentences and grammar:** units and lessons, cheat sheets, sentence tiles, Gimel content
5. **Texts and audio:** reading library, tap-to-translate, audio, listening drills
6. **Accounts and app:** cloud sync, badges and reminders, Dalet and Hey content, App Store version
